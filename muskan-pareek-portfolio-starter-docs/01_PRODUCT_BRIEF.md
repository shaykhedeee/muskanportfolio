# Product Brief

## Product

A premium personal portfolio website for **Muskan Pareek, Interior Designer**, based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.

## Positioning

Muskan should appear as a designer who combines creative interior thinking with technical production and fast modern workflows.

Core positioning:

> Interior designer focused on residential interiors, space planning, modular design, AutoCAD drafting, advanced SketchUp modelling, photorealistic visualization and AI-assisted design workflows.

The website must communicate that she can move from concept to execution rather than only create attractive renders.

## Experience signal

Use **3+ years of experience** as the conservative public-facing experience statement unless the final CV specifies a different approved number.

Professional history available for the site:
- Spacious Venture — Interior Designer / Senior Interior Designer, May 2026–Present
- Freelance Interior Designer & 3D Visualizer — Aug 2025–Apr 2026 (confirm start month before public launch)
- Giftyaari — Founder and Owner, May 2025–Jul 2025
- 12 Square Interiors — Senior Interior Designer, Jun 2025–Sep 2025
- CubeDecors — Interior Designer, Nov 2022–Aug 2024
- The Design Palette — Interior Design Intern, May 2022–Jul 2022
- B.Sc. Interior Design, INIFD Institute, 2019–2022

## User outcomes

### Recruiter / employer
Within 20–40 seconds, they should understand:
- what Muskan does,
- what type of interior work she is strongest at,
- whether her 2D/3D/furniture/moodboard quality is strong,
- her professional experience,
- how to contact her or download a resume.

### Potential client
Within 60–90 seconds, they should see:
- finished visual quality,
- ability to plan and detail spaces,
- process from brief to execution,
- relevant project categories,
- a clear contact path.

- warm

## Visual metaphor

A working interior designer’s desk translated into a refined website:
- sample swatches,
- traced curves,
- floor-plan lines,
- material snippets,
- taped notes,
- arches,
- paper edges,
- shadows from leaves,
- furniture silhouettes,
- sunlight.

The site should feel composed and art-directed, not chaotic.

## Success criteria

### Visual
- Looks custom at 1920×1080, not template-derived.
- Every full-screen section can stand alone as a portfolio slide.
- Interior imagery remains the visual hero.
- Yellow appears in intentional focal points.

### UX
- One deliberate scroll gesture = one major section on desktop.
- Selected Projects handles its own internal project sequence before exiting.
- Section progress is always visible.
- Keyboard, touch and reduced-motion users are fully supported.
- Project and category pages have stable URLs.

### Employer usability
- Resume/CV is reachable in one click from header or About/Experience.
- “My Style of Work” communicates 2D, 3D, furniture and moodboard quality without opening case studies.
- Software/capability list is concise and searchable.

## Key content pillars

2. Space planning
3. Modular furniture and storage
4. AutoCAD drawings
5. Advanced SketchUp modelling
6. Photorealistic 3D visualization
7. AI-assisted design and visualization
8. Furniture design
9. Material / laminate / finish selection
