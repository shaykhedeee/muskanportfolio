# 14. Final UX Principle

The website should feel memorable because of **composition, typography, photography, motion and detail**, not because it contains a large number of features.

The final experience should be:

- visually expressive
- simple to understand
- fast
- recruiter-friendly
- project-focused
- easy to navigate
- easy to download
- easy to share
- strong on desktop
- natural on mobile

The 100vh homepage behaves like an interactive presentation. The catalogue and project pages behave like professional portfolios that can be studied at the visitor's own pace.

The most important actions on the entire website are deliberately simple:

**View the work. Understand the designer. Download the portfolio. Download the resume. Connect on LinkedIn.**
